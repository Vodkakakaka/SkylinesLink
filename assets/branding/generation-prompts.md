## Exact prompt

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: original promotional hero artwork for SkylinesLink (天际互联), an independent AI-to-city MCP project. Create one exceptionally polished wide panoramic 2:1 composition, suitable for a GitHub project cover and a game mod marketplace. This must be an ORIGINAL CONCEPT ILLUSTRATION, not a game screenshot or fake software UI. A calm sophisticated miniature contemporary city, viewed at a 35 degree aerial angle, with coherent low and mid rise blocks, a few clean taller forms, parks and waterways; a single broad flowing route links two distinct city neighborhoods into one legible continuous network. The visual idea is 'a city connected through thoughtful intent', conveyed by physical streets and two restrained pale mint signal ribbons that follow the route, no floating interface cards or dense constellation dots. City occupies the right two thirds; leave quiet dark negative space on the left for separately typeset project branding. Crisp architectural model forms with subtle tactile shading, restrained editorial 3D illustration rather than glossy sci fi. Palette: deep midnight navy #081B26, warm off white buildings #EDF4F4, mint #4DE1B4 and icy cyan #60C8E8 accents; a very small warm amber civic focal point is acceptable. Refined indirect light, beautiful spatial hierarchy, excellent legibility at thumbnail size, clean silhouette, no clutter. No text, no letters, no wordmark, no badges, no logos, no watermarks, no robots, no brain or AI chip, no people, no brand marks from Cities Skylines, Paradox, OpenAI or DeepSeek. Do not imitate any existing game's rendering or promotional art. No HUD, cursor, performance metrics, photograph, screenshot, or claims of product capabilities. Full opaque background.
